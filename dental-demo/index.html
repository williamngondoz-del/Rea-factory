FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Can't find what you need? Call us — a real person always answers.

Do you accept medical insurance?

Yes — we work with most major medical aid schemes and will check your cover before treatment.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Professional whitening is safe and supervised. We assess your teeth first and tailor the strength to avoid sensitivity.

What if I'm anxious about treatment?

You're not alone. We offer longer appointments, stop signals, calming music and sedation options.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

We keep same-day slots for emergencies, including weekends. Call and we'll see you as quickly as possible.

How often should I have a check-up?

Every six months for most people. We'll recommend a schedule that suits your oral health.
